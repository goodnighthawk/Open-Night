# Open Night v1.3

This release folds trusted player reports 52–59 into one coherent city-flow and visual-consolidation pass.

- Civilian sheet vehicles are normalized to the runtime heading contract, while emergency, bus, utility, truck, van, and taxi fallbacks remain available.
- Primary roads now have five physical grid cells of clearance for six lanes; the central highway has seven.
- Intersections use a clean, consistent marking vocabulary and pavement infill replaces stretched curb bars.
- Traffic keeps three usable lanes per direction, sustained recovery, collision avoidance, and role-aware signals.
- Ten distributed supplier/buyer characters are authoritative stationary NPCs, not floating client markers.
- Ambient pedestrians may pass through one another while still reacting to horns, cars, and crossing signals.
- Road-edge lamps are three times larger, base-anchored on sidewalks, and overhang the road with blue-violet light.
- Tyre squeal requires a sustained high-speed turn and has a four-second cooldown.
- The deterministic grunge-neon grade unifies asphalt, pavement, markings, lamps, and warm accents.

Wire protocol and public release version: `1.3`.
